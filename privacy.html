Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy covers the Sigma Moments website and the Sigma Moments app registered with TikTok for Developers (the "app").

What the app collects

The app signs in to one TikTok account, our own (@sigma.moment._). From TikTok it receives that account's basic profile (its ID and display name) and the access tokens needed to upload videos. It uses them only to upload our own finished videos to that account's TikTok inbox.

The app does not sign in other users and does not collect, store or share any information about other TikTok users or viewers.

Where it is kept

The tokens are stored on our own production computer, readable only by our account, and are never sold or shared. Revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings deletes its ability to use them.

This website

This website is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or tracking of its own. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
